Little Kerala

An interactive 3D miniature diorama of a fictional Kerala neighbourhood. Features 12 tiled-roof houses and shops (including Nair's Chaya Kada tea shop), a village pond, swaying coconut palms, a bus shelter with waiting passengers, and left-hand traffic led by a red-and-cream KSRTC-style bus.

Guided Showcase Tour

Runs an automated 45-second presentation cycling through overview, bus tracking, night illumination, and tropical monsoon rain.
